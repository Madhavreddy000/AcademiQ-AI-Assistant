OFFICIAL UNIVERSITY STUDENT HANDBOOK & LIFE GUIDE
Academic Year: 2025 - 2026
Published by the Office of the Dean of Student Affairs

1. CAMPUS RESIDENTIAL & HOSTEL POLICIES:
1.1 Curfew and Entry Timings:
Hostel resident gates close strictly at 10:00 PM on weekdays and 10:30 PM on weekends and public holidays. Late entry requires biometric recording and warden notification.
1.2 Quiet Hours:
Quiet hours are observed across all residence halls between 11:00 PM and 6:00 AM to facilitate study and rest.

2. CENTRAL LIBRARY & DIGITAL RESOURCE USAGE:
2.1 Borrowing Privileges:
Undergraduate students may borrow up to 4 books simultaneously for a duration of 14 calendar days. Renewals may be performed online up to twice if no active holds exist.
2.2 Overdue Penalties:
An overdue fine of $0.50 per book per day is charged for unreturned volumes. Digital library access through IEEE Xplore, ACM Digital Library, and SpringerLink is available campus-wide and via institutional VPN.

3. GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL & ANTI-RAGGING DIRECTIVE:
3.1 Zero-Tolerance on Harassment:
The university maintains a strict zero-tolerance policy towards any form of harassment, ragging, or bullying. Violations lead to immediate police reporting and expulsion under the National Anti-Ragging Framework.
3.2 24/7 Helpline & Counseling Services:
Students in need of psychological counseling or academic stress support can reach out to the Student Wellness Center (Room 204, Student Union) or call the 24/7 helpline at 1-800-UNIV-AID.
