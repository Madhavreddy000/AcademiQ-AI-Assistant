UNIVERSITY ACADEMIC REGULATIONS & EXAMINATION HANDBOOK
Academic Session: 2025 - 2026
Approved by the Academic Council

CHAPTER 1: ACADEMIC CREDITS AND COURSE REGISTRATION
1.1 Minimum and Maximum Credit Limits:
Full-time undergraduate engineering students must register for a minimum of 16 credits and a maximum of 26 credits in any regular semester.
1.2 Course Add/Drop Period:
Students may adjust course registrations during the first two weeks of the semester with the explicit approval of their Faculty Advisor.

CHAPTER 4: ATTENDANCE ELIGIBILITY FOR SEMESTER EXAMINATIONS
4.1 Mandatory Requirement:
Every registered student must maintain a minimum of 75% overall attendance across theory lectures, tutorials, and practical laboratory sessions in each registered subject to be eligible to appear for the End-Semester Examination.
4.2 Condonation of Attendance Shortage on Medical Grounds:
The Dean (Academic Affairs) is empowered to condone attendance shortages between 65% and 74% strictly upon presentation of verified medical certification from a recognized hospital or government medical officer, or for official university representation in national sports or academic symposiums.
A prescribed condonation fee of $25 per course applies. Applications must be lodged within 7 calendar days of returning to campus.
4.3 Course Detention:
Students with attendance strictly below 65% in any subject are categorically detained in that course. No condonation is permissible under any circumstances. Detained students must re-register and re-attend classes when the course is offered in the subsequent academic session.

CHAPTER 5: EXAMINATION CONDUCT AND HALL TICKET ISSUANCE
5.1 Hall Ticket Prerequisites:
Hall tickets are released digitally 72 hours prior to the commencement of examinations via the student portal. To access the hall ticket, students must clear all tuition dues, library books, and have verified attendance eligibility.
5.2 Entry and Physical Verification:
Candidates must produce the printed physical Hall Ticket along with their official University Identity Card. Entry into the examination center is prohibited after 15 minutes from the scheduled start time.
5.3 Academic Malpractice & Code of Integrity:
Possession of unapproved written material, mobile phones, smartwatches, or programmable electronic devices inside the examination hall constitutes an offense of academic malpractice. The Examination Disciplinary Committee may cancel the entire semester examination performance and suspend the candidate for up to two semesters.

CHAPTER 7: 10-POINT LETTER GRADING SCALE & CGPA
7.1 Letter Grades and Grade Points:
- O (Outstanding, 90% – 100%): Grade Point 10
- A+ (Excellent, 80% – 89%): Grade Point 9
- A (Very Good, 70% – 79%): Grade Point 8
- B+ (Good, 60% – 69%): Grade Point 7
- B (Above Average, 55% – 59%): Grade Point 6
- C (Average, 50% – 54%): Grade Point 5
- P (Pass, 40% – 49%): Grade Point 4
- F (Fail / Absent, Below 40%): Grade Point 0
7.2 Degree Award Minimum Criteria:
A minimum cumulative grade point average (CGPA) of 5.0 is mandatory for the conferment of the undergraduate engineering degree.
